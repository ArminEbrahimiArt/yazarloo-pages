About

Rahman Yazarloo

"A boy with a pencil case."

That’s how it started, and at heart, that's who I still am. For two decades, I built worlds you couldn't touch. But when pixels weren't enough, I needed to pull my imagination into the real world.

Today, as a Visual Narrator, my work lives where the digital meets the tangible. Whether I’m directing an animation or sculpting with my bare hands, my mission is giving physical weight to wild ideas. Let’s build something extraordinary.
